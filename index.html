About

In every photo I take, I look for the simplest, most natural aspect of the frame.

I work with available light and honest colour. No heavy grading, no props that were not already in the room. That approach came out of shooting cafes and bars on slow mornings, waiting for the light to land on a table rather than building it with flash.

My work covers three areas: portraits, hospitality interiors and travel. Hotels and restaurants hire me for imagery that shows a room as a guest actually finds it. Magazines and brands hire me for portraits and location work that still reads well in five years.
